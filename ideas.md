# Corextend Technologies Website Design Brainstorm

## Design Approach Selected: **Premium Tech Minimalism with Warm Accents**

### Design Movement
**Contemporary Enterprise Minimalism** — inspired by high-end SaaS platforms and luxury tech brands that balance cutting-edge aesthetics with approachability. Think Stripe, Figma, and modern consulting firms.

### Core Principles
1. **Precision Over Decoration** — Every element serves a purpose. Clean lines, generous whitespace, and deliberate hierarchy guide the eye naturally.
2. **Warmth in Minimalism** — Avoid cold, sterile tech aesthetics. Incorporate warm gold/amber accents and soft gradients to humanize the interface and convey trust.
3. **Narrative Flow** — The page tells a story: problem → solution → proof → action. Each section builds momentum toward the CTA.
4. **Subtle Motion** — Micro-interactions and entrance animations feel natural and premium, never jarring or excessive.

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary Palette**: Deep navy/slate (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`) as the foundation, representing stability and expertise
- **Accent Color**: Warm gold/amber (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`) for CTAs and highlights, conveying value and warmth
- **Neutrals**: Soft grays and off-whites for breathing room and contrast
- **Emotional Intent**: The navy conveys enterprise-grade reliability; the gold signals premium value and opportunity

### Layout Paradigm
- **Asymmetric Hero** — Text on left, hero image on right with slight parallax on scroll
- **Alternating Section Alignment** — Services alternate between left-aligned and right-aligned layouts to create visual rhythm
- **Strategic Whitespace** — Generous padding and breathing room between sections to emphasize quality over quantity
- **Sticky Navigation** — Minimal top nav that stays visible, reinforcing brand presence

### Signature Elements
1. **Gold Accent Lines** — Subtle horizontal or diagonal lines in gold that frame key sections and create visual continuity
2. **Card-Based Service Display** — Services presented as elegant cards with hover lift effects and gold borders on hover
3. **Gradient Overlays** — Subtle navy-to-transparent gradients on image sections to ensure text readability and visual depth

### Interaction Philosophy
- **Hover States** — Cards lift slightly with a soft shadow; links gain a gold underline
- **Smooth Scrolling** — Gentle fade-in animations as sections come into view
- **CTA Emphasis** — Primary buttons have gold backgrounds with navy text; secondary buttons have navy borders with gold text on hover

### Animation
- **Entrance Animations** — Sections fade in and slide up slightly as they enter the viewport (100-200ms duration)
- **Hover Lift** — Service cards and buttons lift 4-8px on hover with a soft shadow transition
- **Scroll Parallax** — Hero image moves slightly slower than the text for depth perception
- **Underline Animation** — Links gain an animated gold underline on hover (left-to-right direction)

### Typography System
- **Display Font** — `Sora` (modern, geometric, tech-forward) for headlines and hero text
- **Body Font** — `Inter` (clean, highly legible) for body copy and UI elements
- **Hierarchy**:
  - H1: Sora, 48-56px, bold (hero headline)
  - H2: Sora, 36-40px, semibold (section headers)
  - H3: Sora, 24-28px, medium (subsections)
  - Body: Inter, 16px, regular (content)
  - Small: Inter, 14px, regular (captions and metadata)

---

## Implementation Notes
- All images will be positioned strategically to avoid overwhelming the minimalist aesthetic
- The user-provided images (hero, custom software, product management) will be integrated with subtle overlays to maintain brand consistency
- FAQs will be presented as an accordion component with smooth expand/collapse animations
- Contact form will be clean and minimal, with inline validation and success messaging
